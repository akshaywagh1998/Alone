SCORING SYSTEM:
═══════════════

6 DIMENSIONS EVALUATED:

1. Length & Depth (0-20 points)
   Checks if your answer is substantive and detailed

2. Evidence & Facts (0-25 points)
   Looks for specific dates, statistics, laws, and cases

3. Balance & Nuance (0-20 points)
   Checks for multiple perspectives and counterarguments

4. India Context (0-15 points)
   Ensures India-specific relevance

5. Structural Clarity (0-10 points)
   Evaluates paragraph organization

6. Logical Flow (0-10 points)
   Checks for smooth transitions and coherence

TOTAL SCORE: 0-100 points

STATUS LEVELS:
├─ 80-100: EXCELLENT (exam-ready)
├─ 60-79:  GOOD (strong, needs refinement)
├─ 40-59:  FAIR (core content present, needs work)
└─ <40:    NEEDS WORK (significant revision required)
