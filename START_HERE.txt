UNDERSTANDING THE SCORING:
══════════════════════════

Your answers are scored on 6 dimensions:

[1] LENGTH & DEPTH (0-20 points)
    → Is your answer substantive? (750+ words preferred)

[2] EVIDENCE & FACTS (0-25 points)
    → Do you cite dates, laws, cases, statistics?

[3] BALANCE & NUANCE (0-20 points)
    → Do you show multiple perspectives?

[4] INDIA CONTEXT (0-15 points)
    → Is it relevant to Indian affairs?

[5] STRUCTURAL CLARITY (0-10 points)
    → Is it well organized with clear paragraphs?

[6] LOGICAL FLOW (0-10 points)
    → Do ideas connect smoothly?

TOTAL SCORE: 0-100 points

Status Levels:
├─ 80-100: EXCELLENT (exam-ready)
├─ 60-79:  GOOD (strong foundation)
├─ 40-59:  FAIR (core present, needs work)
└─ <40:    NEEDS WORK (substantial revision)

PRACTICAL TIPS FOR SUCCESS:
═══════════════════════════

1. CREATE A TRACKING SHEET
   Keep a simple list:
   - Date
   - Topic name
   - First score
   - Revision score
   - Final score
   This shows your improvement pattern

2. READ ACTUAL UPSC ANSWERS CONCURRENTLY
   The portal gives feedback but also:
   - Read NCERT books
   - Follow news daily
   - Solve actual previous year questions
   - Read exemplar answers from coaching materials

3. TIME YOUR WRITING
   Start: Writing without time pressure (perfect content)
   Later: Time yourself to 45 minutes per answer
   Final: Fully exam conditions (3 questions in 3 hours)

4. BUILD YOUR EXAMPLES LIBRARY
   For each topic:
   - Note 3-5 solid examples
   - Add supporting data/years
   - Create personal notes
   - Review weekly

5. USE THE FEEDBACK SYSTEMATICALLY
   Don't just verify once:
   - Get feedback
   - Read all 6 dimensions
   - Revise weakest area
   - Re-verify
   - Repeat 2-3 times per topic

6. MIX PAPERS IN YOUR PRACTICE
   Don't do all Paper I, then Paper II
   Mix: 2 from Paper I, 2 from Paper II, 2 from Paper III, 2 from Paper IV
   This prevents topic fatigue and improves retention

TROUBLESHOOTING:
═════════════════

Portal won't load?
  1. Check both files are in same folder
  2. Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Del)
  3. Try different browser
  4. Check browser console (F12) for errors

Topics missing?
  1. Hard refresh browser (Ctrl+F5)
  2. Verify syllabus_data.json file exists
  3. Check JSON file isn't corrupted

Verification button doesn't work?
  1. Write at least 50 words
  2. Make sure text box has focus
  3. Check browser console for errors
  4. Try in different browser

Scoring seems wrong?
  1. Review the 6 scoring dimensions
  2. Compare your answer with examples
  3. Read feedback carefully
  4. Remember: it's guidance, not gospel
