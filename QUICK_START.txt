SCORING EXPLAINED:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The portal checks your answer on 6 dimensions:

[1] Length & Depth (0-20 points)
    ✓ Is your answer detailed and substantive?
    → Include 750+ words, multiple paragraphs

[2] Evidence & Facts (0-25 points)
    ✓ Do you use dates, statistics, laws, cases?
    → Add specific examples: "Bharatnatyam", "Article 45", "1989"

[3] Balance & Nuance (0-20 points)
    ✓ Do you show multiple perspectives?
    → Use: "However...", "On the other hand...", "Despite..."

[4] India Context (0-15 points)
    ✓ Is it relevant to India?
    → Reference Constitution, laws, policies, Indian events

[5] Structure (0-10 points)
    ✓ Is your answer organized?
    → Use 3-5 clear paragraphs with logical flow

[6] Logical Flow (0-10 points)
    ✓ Do ideas connect smoothly?
    → Use: "Therefore", "Thus", "Moreover", "Furthermore"
